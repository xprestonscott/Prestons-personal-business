Most guys I talk to do, and I'm not knocking it. But your site doesn't text anybody back when you're forty feet up a ladder — that's the part I'm talking about. I can leave your site exactly where it is and put this on top of it.

Why is there a monthly fee?

Because there's a monthly service. It's carrying a phone line, sending texts, hosting the site, and me making whatever changes you want. If it were just a file sitting on a server I'd charge you once and disappear — but then it wouldn't do anything for you.

Is this one of those AI chatbots?

No. It's a text from your number saying you missed them and asking what they need. When they write back, you answer. It's your customer and your conversation — nothing pretends to be you.

What if it doesn't get me anything?

Then you'd see that on the dashboard and you'd cancel, and you should. That's exactly why the dashboard exists. I'd rather you be able to check than have to trust me.
