Without a real site you mostly get the people who already knew your name. That's a much smaller group than the people looking for what you do.

An old site makes people wonder if you're still in business. They don't ask — they just go back and pick someone else.

They can't get hold of you

No number they can tap, no form, no hours, no list of what towns you cover. Every extra step loses a few more of them.
